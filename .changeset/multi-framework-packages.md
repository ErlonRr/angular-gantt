Gantry is now available for every framework: `@gantryjs/core` (framework-free logic), `@gantryjs/vanilla` (`<gantry-element>` Web Component), and `@gantryjs/react`, `@gantryjs/vue`, `@gantryjs/svelte` bindings. `@gantryjs/angular` now builds on `@gantryjs/core` and gains column sorting (built-in columns are sortable).
